01 — The story

The person you know.
The person they are.

Chloe Reed is fifteen, living in a town where Friday-night football is practically a civic duty and everybody seems to know everybody. Seven months with Thomas Miller feel like proof that she understands love. Then the small things stop adding up — and Austin Prant, a boy she barely noticed at first, becomes the person willing to tell her what no one else will.

A story about certainty, first love, betrayal, friendship, and the quiet way someone unexpected can become important before you even realize it's happening.

Published chapters lead to Wattpad. The rest stay spoiler-light here until their Thursday comes.

Wellington, Dakota, Christian, Thomas — and one almost forgettable moment with Austin at the front doors. Chloe is certain seven months means she knows Thomas. The chapter quietly asks whether certainty and knowledge are actually the same thing.

Friday night belongs to Wellington football. Thomas is the reason Chloe came, but Austin becomes visible in a new way on the field — focused, serious, and unexpectedly hard not to watch.

Read Chapter 2 on Wattpad ↗

Thomas tells Austin that Chloe is good at chemistry. What should be ordinary tutoring creates an easy new rhythm between them.

The week feels normal on the surface. Small details begin to sit strangely beside the certainty Chloe wants to keep.

Chloe is welcomed into Thomas's family and feels as though she has been given proof that their relationship belongs somewhere permanent.

Messages get shorter. Chloe notices, explains, excuses and waits. Nothing is clearly wrong — which gives her room to keep explaining it.

A Saturday of waiting, a Christmas frame, and small reasons to think about Austin. Thomas is just having a bad week. Bad weeks end. Don't they?

After school, Austin asks Chloe if they can talk about Thomas. One message is enough to make seven months suddenly look different.

Chloe starts sorting through all the small things she had explained away. Dakota stays. Later, Austin does too.
