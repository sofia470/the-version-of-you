01 — The story

Some things
aren't meant
to stay the same.

Chloe Reed thought she knew what love was. Seven months with Thomas Miller had made her believe she had found the person she was supposed to be with.

But when everything falls apart, the last person she expects to get closer to is Austin Prant.

Maybe the version of someone you love isn't always the person they really are.

02 — The characters

Meet them.

01

Fifteen. Ninth grade. A girl who thought she knew the person she loved, until she had to discover who she was without him.

02

Austin Prant

Sixteen. Tenth grade. Quietly observant, surrounded by people but focused on his own world. Until Chloe becomes part of it.

03

Thomas Miller

Seventeen. Eleventh grade. Chloe's boyfriend for seven months, and the person whose choices change everything.
